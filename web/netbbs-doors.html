For callers & SysOps

One door menu. Plenty of ways to play.

Open Doors on your NetBBS node to see what its SysOp offers. Voidrunner, War Dialer and Retro Trivia ship with NetBBS and can be registered from the SysOp’s game gallery. Blacksite and Lunar Lander are separate projects in development and are not part of the bundled games.

Alongside native games, NetBBS supports classic DOS doors through DOSBox-X, doors for other operating systems in a virtual machine on POSIX hosts, and remote services such as DoorParty and BBSLink. Installation and compatibility depend on the game and host.

SysOps can find setup templates, supported configurations and checks in the door setup guide. The user handbook explains the caller’s side.
