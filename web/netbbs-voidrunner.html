The game

A ship of your own. A galaxy to make a living in.

In the tradition of Elite and Trade Wars, Voidrunner puts you in charge of a small ship and a career that carries on between calls. Learn the markets, chart unfamiliar systems and grow from a lone trader into a captain with a crew and a place in the galaxy.

Make the next run count

Buy where goods are cheap and sell where demand is high. Fuel, cargo space and a damaged hull all compete for your credits.

Choose your work

Take delivery, escort, survey and bounty contracts. Hire specialists, build faction ties and fight or talk your way past raiders.

Keep a career worth remembering

Return to the same pilot, upgrade your ship and earn achievements. Retire a successful career into the Hall of Fame.

At the keyboard

Inside Voidrunner

Build a space-trading career across a 48-system galaxy. Haul cargo, take contracts, recruit a crew and decide how much danger your next jump is worth.

Play on NetBBS

Open Doors on a NetBBS node that offers Voidrunner and choose the game. Your pilot is saved between visits. Each caller has their own career.

The game is bundled with NetBBS. A node’s SysOp decides which doors callers can access. Connect using Telnet, SSH or the browser terminal, then open the door menu.

For installation and supported host configurations, read the door setup guide or the game’s own README.
