The game

Ten exchanges. Rival crews. One shared scene.

War Dialer turns the dial-up underground into a shared strategy game. Every caller plays in the same world, but rivals do not have to be online together. Your next visit is a chance to build the crew, work the network and see what changed while you were away. Its screens glow like the phosphor monitors of the era, and from 100 columns up they spread out to fill a wide terminal.

Work the ring

Compete for control of ten phone exchanges. The switchboard shows the network and who holds each part of it.

Build your crew

Take contract jobs, earn cash and rank, and weigh your odds before making a move. Raids and the risk of a bust keep success from being comfortable.

Play the season

Your crew lives in a world shared with the other callers. Seasons give the competition a finish line and medals a place in its history.

At the keyboard

Inside War Dialer

Lead a crew through a fictional BBS network. Compete with other callers for ten phone exchanges, take contracts and build your standing across a season.

Play on NetBBS

Open Doors on a NetBBS node that offers War Dialer. Return to the same node to continue in its shared world; other callers can make their moves between your visits.

The game is bundled with NetBBS. A node’s SysOp decides which doors callers can access. Connect using Telnet, SSH or the browser terminal, then open the door menu.

For installation and supported host configurations, read the door setup guide or the game’s own README.
