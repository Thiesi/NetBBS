Design & planning

Lunar Lander is at the design and planning stage and is not playable yet. Everything described here is planned. The artwork is project concept art, not a gameplay screenshot.

The plan

Read the drift. Watch the fuel. Make both feet count.

Lunar Lander is a precision flying game being designed for the NetBBS terminal. A small spacecraft, a dwindling fuel supply and an unforgiving Moon turn a short descent into a careful balance of drift, speed and fuel.

Fly the descent

The plan centres on deterministic lunar physics and precise landing checks. Read your drift, manage your descent and put both feet on the pad.

Learn, then test yourself

A training basin, three authored challenges and a seeded daily challenge are planned, with pilot records and leaderboards for completed safe landings.

A Moon in ANSI colour

Rich terminal landscapes, stars, a distant Earth and illuminated pads are the visual direction. Larger terminals are intended to reveal more scenery.
